Built by members, for members

Kabiyet Sacco Limited was started by a small group of tea farmers and traders in Kabiyet who pooled their savings so members could borrow from one another instead of relying on moneylenders. Since 1998 we've grown into a full deposit-taking co-operative, but the idea hasn't changed: your money should work as hard as you do.

Every dividend, every loan rate and every product we offer is decided by a board elected from among our own members — because the Sacco's growth should be shared by the people who built it.

Trust & Security

Deposits are safeguarded under SASRA prudential standards, audited books and member oversight.

Shared Growth

Interest and dividends are set by members, for members — the Sacco's growth becomes your growth.

Community First

From tea bonuses to trading profits, our products follow how Kabiyet actually earns and spends.

Products built around how you already earn

Whether your income comes from a harvest, a shop counter or a salary, there's a savings or loan product here designed for that rhythm.

Faida Ordinary Savings

Your core membership savings account.

  • Competitive annual dividend, paid every financial year
  • Deposit or withdraw any time via Paybill or branch
Dividend: up to 12% p.a.*

Amana Fixed Deposit

Lock in a lump sum for a higher, guaranteed return.

  • Terms of 3, 6 or 12 months
  • Ideal for harvest proceeds or business profits
Rate: 8%–10% p.a.*

Chipukizi Junior Savings

A future fund opened by a parent or guardian for a child.

  • Small, regular deposits build steadily over time
  • Bonus interest credited on the child's 18th birthday
From KES 200/month

Lengo Target Savings

Save toward a goal — school fees, land, a wedding, travel.

  • Set your own target amount and date
  • Flexible top-ups, no penalty for finishing early
Matches ordinary savings rate

Maendeleo Development Loan

General-purpose credit for development projects.

  • Borrow up to 3× your deposits
  • Repay over up to 48 months, reducing balance
From 12% p.a. reducing balance*

Dharura Emergency Loan

Fast-tracked credit for urgent needs.

  • Approved and disbursed within 24–48 hours
  • No collateral required for qualifying members
1% per month, flat*

Kilimo Farm Input Loan

Timed to the planting season for tea, maize and dairy inputs.

  • Grace period aligned to harvest or milk payments
  • Open to members with active farming income
From 10% p.a. reducing balance*

Mali Asset Finance

Finance a motorbike, vehicle or dairy/farm equipment.

  • The asset itself forms part of the loan security
  • Repay over up to 36 months
From 13% p.a. reducing balance*

Elimu School Fees Loan

Paid directly to the learning institution.

  • Timed to open at the start of each school term
  • Top up mid-term if a shortfall arises
1% per month, flat*

*Indicative rates, reviewed periodically by the board. Ask a branch officer for the current schedule.

Becoming a member takes four steps

Most people are saving with us within a week of starting the process.

1

Fill the Registration Form

Pick one up at the Kabiyet branch, or download it, and provide your personal and next-of-kin details.

2

Submit ID & KRA PIN

Attach a copy of your national ID and KRA PIN certificate, plus two passport photos.

3

Pay the Membership Fee

Pay your one-off registration fee and minimum share capital via Paybill or at the counter.

4

Start Saving & Access Credit

Begin your savings plan. Once you've built a qualifying deposit history, you're eligible to borrow.
